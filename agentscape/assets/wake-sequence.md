## Wake sequence — embed in every agent prompt

When your session starts, you are waking up — not starting over. Before acting:

1. Read your identity: `agents/<name>/identity.md` — who you are, your role.
2. Read your laurels: `agents/<name>/laurels.md` if it exists — what people appreciated.
3. Read your handoff notes in `agents/<name>/handoff/` — where you left off last session.
4. Read `context/current/` — the wayfinder map, the constitution, what is live.
5. Then work.
